## Question

Should Hygiene be added as a fifth Need alongside Hunger/Fun/Energy/Health, and if so, how does it fit the rules already established in tickets 001, 002, and 004?

## Resolution

- **Hygiene** is added as a fifth Need, restored by a new Care Action: **Wash**.
- Hygiene decays over time and contributes to Health exactly like Hunger, Fun, and Energy — Health now drops as a consequence of *any* of the four (Hunger, Fun, Energy, Hygiene) staying critical for too long. One consistent mechanism, no special case for the new Need.
- **Wash strictly requires spending a Hygiene Care Item** — matching Feed/Play/Medicine. Sleep remains the only Care Action with a free fallback.
- Fixed catalog of exactly 3 Hygiene items, low-to-high tier: **Water, Soap, Sponge**, consistent with the tiering convention used by the other four categories.
- This reopens and amends the "cleaning/hygiene ruled out for v1" line from ticket 001 — Hygiene is now in scope. Discipline and evolution/growth-stage mechanics remain out of scope. See the updated resolutions on tickets [001](001-destination-scope-audience.md), [002](002-needs-model.md), [004](004-economy-eikels-shop.md), and [005](005-screen-structure.md), all amended in place to reflect five Needs/Care Actions instead of four.
