## Question

What triggers Death, how does revival work, and does Death affect the child's ability to keep learning?

## Resolution

- **Death trigger**: sustained zero Health (Health staying at zero for a sustained period — exact grace period is a tunable constant, see ticket 007).
- **Revival**: only a parent can revive, via a new action in the existing PIN-gated Parent Portal. Available any time, no cooldown or limit.
- Reviving restores Needs to a **partial**, not full, level — a death isn't a free full reset.
- **Death never blocks** the child's ability to do exercises or earn XP/Eikels. The care mechanic adds motivation on top of the core app; it must never gate core learning progress.
